ENVIRONMENT & LANDSCAPE

FRANZ_JOSEF_LAND_ARCHIPELAGO
Description: A remote Arctic archipelago in the Arctic Ocean, part of Russian territory, characterized by its rugged terrain, icy landscapes, and a majority of its area covered by glaciers. It consists of over 192 islands, featuring steep cliffs, icy shores, and expansive glacial fields.
Mandatory features:
- thick glacier coverage with visible ice sheets and cliffs
- ice and snow-dominated environments with sparse vegetation and tundra
- presence of drifting icebergs and snow-covered surfaces
Never:
- densely populated or urban structures
- modern urban architecture or lush, tropical vegetation

CAPE_FLIGELY
Description: The northernmost point of Franz Josef Land and the entire Russian territory, located on Rudolf Island. It is a crucial landmark associated with polar exploration.
Mandatory features:
- stark, rocky terrain with a clear view of surrounding icy waters
- dominated by a polar climate with minimal vegetation
Never:
- any kind of modern infrastructure or habitation

QUIET_BAY
Description: Known as Tikhaya Bay, this sheltered inlet on Hooker Island was once a bustling hub for Soviet polar exploration. It features deserted wooden structures, a flagpole, and an airplane hangar from the early Soviet polar station.
Mandatory features:
- abandoned wooden polar station buildings and a Soviet-era flagpole
- an airplane hangar, iconic of early Soviet polar exploration
- atmosphere of stillness, frozen in time by the Arctic climate
Never:
- overt signs of contemporary renovations or modern construction materials

TIKHAYA_BUKHTA
Description: A small, closed bay on Hooker Island, holding several historic wooden buildings, including the first permanent Soviet polar station, now an open-air museum.
Mandatory features:
- historic wooden buildings, preserved as an open-air museum
- Arctic Ocean setting with harsh climatic conditions
- isolated position on an Arctic island
Never:
- modern infrastructure or urban development

WILDLIFE & FAUNA

POLAR_BEAR
Description: The Arctic's iconic carnivore, frequently found in the Franz Josef Land region, recognized for its massive size and snowy white fur.
Mandatory features:
- prominent white fur adapted to the Arctic's cold environment
- presence around icy and snowy landscapes
Never:
- confused with brown or grizzly bears

ATLANTIC_WALRUS
Description: A large marine mammal native to the Arctic waters around Franz Josef Land, characterized by its long ivory tusks, whiskers, and massive body size.
Mandatory features:
- long ivory tusks and whiskers
- large, social groups on ice floes or shores
Never:
- mistaken for seals or other marine mammals

ARCTIC_FOX

- winter white coat or summer brown/grey coat

- towering basalt cliff with visible bird nests
Never:
- modern structures or signs of human habitat
